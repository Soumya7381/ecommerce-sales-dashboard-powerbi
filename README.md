# 📊 Madhav Ecommerce Sales Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
The Madhav Ecommerce Sales Dashboard is an interactive business intelligence project built using Power BI. It analyzes sales performance, customer behavior, and profit trends to support data-driven decision-making.

---

## ❗ Problem Statement
E-commerce businesses often face challenges in:
- Tracking sales and profit across regions  
- Identifying top customers and products  
- Analyzing monthly performance trends  
- Understanding payment preferences  

This dashboard solves these problems by providing clear, interactive insights.

---

## 🎯 Objective
- Monitor sales, profit, and quantity  
- Analyze monthly profit trends  
- Identify top customers and states  
- Understand category and payment distribution  
- Enable dynamic filtering  

---

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- 💰 Total Sales Amount: 438K  
- 📈 Total Profit: 37K  
- 📦 Total Quantity Sold: 5615  
- 📊 Average Order Value (AOV): 121K  

---

## 🎛️ Filters & Slicers
- Quarter-wise filter (Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4)  
- State-wise filtering  
- Fully interactive dashboard  

---

## 📈 Visualizations Used
- Column Chart: Profit by Month, Sales by Customer  
- Bar Chart: Profit by Sub-Category, Sales by State  
- Donut Charts: Quantity by Category, Quantity by Payment Mode  

---

## 📁 Dataset Information
The dataset includes:
- Customer details  
- Sales (Amount, Profit, Quantity)  
- Product categories and sub-categories  
- Payment modes  
- State-wise data  

Data preprocessing was done using Microsoft Excel.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights
- Clothing category has the highest quantity share  
- COD is the most preferred payment mode  
- Some months show negative profit  
- Top customers contribute significantly to revenue  
- Maharashtra and Madhya Pradesh lead in sales
